Owned by the Community, Driven by Care

Providing official stakes to community members and quality health services to Metkei.

Our journey began back in 2015 as a critical medical outreach initiative, returning annually to run free medical camps and provide desperate care to the local community. This regular outreach continued faithfully until the arrival of COVID-19 in 2021, when necessary bans on public gatherings disrupted our mobile service style.

Rather than stopping, this challenge highlighted a deeper need: a permanent shelter and treatment foundation for identified patients. To fulfill this vision, German Doctors stepped forward to support our community, purchasing dedicated land and financing the construction of a permanent facility layout.

Construction moved forward seamlessly, culminating in the completion of our modern hospital framework by January 2025. Today, Metkei Community Health Centre is fully operational—built for the community, run by the community, and managed locally by our dedicated directors, continuing our core tradition of targeted local outreach.

AMPATH

Provides crucial technical support, management expertise for chronic conditions like HIV, Hypertension, and Diabetes, and ensures consistent drug access via a specialized revolving funds pharmacy model.

German Doctors

Funded our structural baseline mission from the ground up, purchasing the physical land plot and funding construction for our Outpatient Department (OPD) clinic rooms.

County Government

The County Department of Health coordinates essential policy leadership and healthcare guidance while supplying vital vaccines, clinical tablets, and regular medical mentorship.

The Community

Local community members drive our governing leadership structure while actively utilizing our treatment services, ensuring lasting grassroots ownership and long-term facility sustainability.

Primary Medical Care

General Outpatients services, full-service pharmacy dispensing, and laboratory diagnostics.

Maternal & Child Health

Comprehensive Antenatal Care (ANC) for pregnant mothers, safe Maternity services, and routine infant immunization.

HIV & Care Programs

Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission (PMTCT) services and dedicated drug dispensing for HIV-positive patients.

Specialized & Community Care

Point-of-Care Ultrasound diagnostics and active community health outreach via Community Health Promoter (CHPs) services.

Free Medical Camps

Organizing strategic, zero-cost medical outreach camps to deliver specialized checkups, screening, and diagnostic relief directly to community doorsteps.
